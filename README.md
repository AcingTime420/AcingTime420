## Acing IU: Genesis

[Acing IU: Genesis](https://github.com/AcingTime420/Acing-IU-Genesis) explores a central question:

> How do people stay in control when AI can act on their behalf?

Genesis is being designed around:

- Human authorization before delegated actions
- Explicit permission, scope, duration, and limits
- Independent policy enforcement
- Revocable authority
- Understandable activity and evidence records
- Zero-trust device and service architecture
- Accurate separation between implemented, simulated, planned, and unavailable capabilities

**IU** means **Interface User**—the point where human intent, authority, and technology meet.

## Engineering Principles

- Security by foundation
- Personal by choice
- Engineered for trust
- Capability claims must be supported by evidence
- AI must not expand its own authority
- Human accountability must remain understandable
